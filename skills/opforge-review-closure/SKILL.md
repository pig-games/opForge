# opForge Review Closure

Connect the original finding to the relevant change and validating reproduction,
test or direct inspection. Distinguish fixed, partial, deferred and superseded
findings. If the original defect still reproduces, do not call it fixed.
Report evidence and remaining uncertainty concisely in the existing review or
conversation. A separate closure artifact or independent approval is not required.
Remove completed reports once lasting information is in maintained docs or tests.
